IP Law

Legal Applicability & Risk

Listing descriptions and photos are protected by copyright (§§ 2, 72 UrhG), while housing portals hold sui generis database rights (§§ 87a–87b UrhG) against systematic scraping.

01
No Bulk Crawling or Shadow DatabaseStrictly user-initiated, single-URL checks avoid any substantial extraction of a portal’s database.
02
Transient Factual Analysis (§§ 44a, 44b UrhG)Reads non-copyrightable raw facts (€ rent, m², Kaution) ephemerally without re-hosting galleries.
03
Referential Trademarks & Deep-LinkingPortal names identify source context descriptively (§ 23 MarkenG); links drive traffic back to the portal.
